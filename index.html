2. Data Analysis Projects

LEARNING PROJECT

Sales Performance Analysis

Problem Statement: A small business needs a clear way to understand its sales performance, identify strong-performing products and compare sales across different locations.

Project Overview: This learning project demonstrates how raw sales data can be cleaned, organized and analyzed in Microsoft Excel to discover useful business patterns.

Dataset: Product, City and Sales records
Tools: Microsoft Excel, Data Cleaning, Excel Tables, Formulas, Sorting, Filtering, Pivot Tables and Charts

Analysis: The data was organized into an Excel table and examined using sorting, filtering and formulas. The project focuses on comparing sales performance by product and location.

Key Findings: The analysis helps identify differences in sales performance and shows how structured data can make important business information easier to understand.

Business Recommendation: Businesses can regularly organize and review their sales data to identify trends, monitor performance and support data-informed decisions.

Project Type: Learning Project

PRACTICE PROJECT

Customer Support Data Analysis

Problem Statement: A business receives different types of customer enquiries and needs a clearer way to organize the information and identify recurring customer issues.

Project Overview: This practice project demonstrates how customer-support records can be organized into meaningful categories and analyzed to identify common enquiry types and service patterns.

Dataset: Customer enquiry types and support records
Tools: Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Data Cleaning, COUNTIF, Sorting, Filtering and Basic Data Analysis

Analysis: Customer enquiries were grouped into categories and reviewed to identify frequently occurring issues and patterns in customer support activity.

Key Findings: Organizing customer-support information makes recurring enquiry types easier to identify and provides a clearer view of areas that may require attention.

Business Recommendation: Businesses can regularly categorize and review customer enquiries to monitor recurring issues and improve the organization of their support process.

Project Type: Practice Project

3. Trading & Coaching

TRADING EDUCATION

Market Analysis & Trade Breakdown

Problem Statement: Beginners can find it difficult to understand why a particular trading setup is considered during market analysis.

Project Overview: This educational project demonstrates a structured approach to studying market conditions, identifying potential setups and explaining the reasoning behind a trade idea.

Topics: Market Structure, Support & Resistance, Trend Analysis, Risk Management and Trade Planning
Analysis Process: Market conditions are reviewed before a potential setup is explained, including the market structure, important price levels, possible entry area, risk level and trade management plan.

Learning Approach: Each example is explained step by step so beginners can understand the reasoning behind the analysis rather than simply following a trading signal.

Learning Goal: Help learners develop a more structured approach to studying charts, planning trades and managing risk.

Project Type: Trading Education

Risk Notice: Educational content only. Trading involves financial risk. Nothing presented here is financial advice or a guarantee of trading results.

SIGNAL BREAKDOWN

Trading Signal Analysis

Problem Statement: Traders, especially beginners, may struggle to understand how a potential trading signal is developed and how risk should be considered before entering a market.

Project Overview: This educational project demonstrates how a potential trade setup can be reviewed using market structure, price levels and basic risk-management principles.

Analysis Includes: Market Structure, Entry Area, Stop Loss, Take Profit, Risk-to-Reward and Trade Planning
Process: A potential setup is reviewed, the reasoning behind the setup is explained, and possible risk-management levels are identified before considering a trade.

Learning Focus: The goal is to help learners understand the analysis behind a potential setup rather than blindly copying a signal.

Risk Management: Every example emphasizes the importance of defining risk, using appropriate position sizing and having a clear trade plan.

Project Type: Trading Education / Signal Analysis

Risk Notice: Examples are for educational purposes only. They are not financial advice and do not guarantee trading results. Trading involves financial risk.
